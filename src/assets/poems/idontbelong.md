Since my teens I have been tied<br/>
To this soul in me who died<br/>
Lone and rusty — left its parts<br/>
Unlike Her that took its sparks—<br/>
In the dark in which it molds<br/>
It took the heart along its folds<br/>
(To which now no one is the better<br/>
Though I went ways to forget her)<br/>
Now old lies this rotting skin<br/>
Staring strangerly at its kin<br/>
Whom did not care or believe<br/>
The one they won't reprieve<br/>
Be it near — Be it far<br/>
From my home towards a star<br/>
Is there no one to save this soul?<br/>
To spark a fire in this coal?<br/>
It's growing old and alone<br/>
Against this world so unknown<br/>
Where all souls are pumping red<br/>
While mine remains dead—<br/>
Is it any fair? To be so dissimilar<br/>
As the reds live all so simpler<br/>
Perhaps I do belong here<br/>
Alone and in the clear<br/>
Be it day — Be it night<br/>
There will never be a light.
