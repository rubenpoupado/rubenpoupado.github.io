This ship of mine is great and heavy<br/>
With plenty of wares to carry,<br/>
It envieth these waves gloomy<br/>
Who crash so violently to me.<br/>
<br/>
I fret not for these endless seas<br/>
For this ship is much sturdy to seize.<br/>
Great waves may come and clash<br/>
Yet they do naught to my stash.<br/>
<br/>
—So God heard and summoneth storms<br/>
Of wrath from His vengeful hordes.<br/>
“Taketh mine vessel, howbeit with mercy—<br/>
Strand me with mine wares sturdy!”<br/>
<br/>
Vile art His lightning casts—<br/>
He rages and breaketh my masts,<br/>
Yet intact remains mine wares,<br/>
These He smites naught in tears!<br/>
<br/>
—Dearest wares of mine,<br/>
In thine memories I confine,<br/>
Taketh me there once more,<br/>
To where I was before.<br/>
<br/>
Gentle and caring was thy heart,<br/>
If only thou didst not depart.<br/>
Was I unworthy to thy stay,<br/>
That thou hadst to cast me away?<br/>
<br/>
Rancid, thou hast become,<br/>
Fooled and left me numb,<br/>
Didst hide from me thy man<br/>
With whom thou ran.<br/>
<br/>
—Fires burneth mine ship now<br/>
and in mine danger, thou—<br/>
And only thou— couldst I see<br/>
Amidst this gloomy sea.<br/>
<br/>
Mine ship burneth down,<br/>
Memories of thee shall drown,<br/>
By this gloomy sea,<br/>
Where I shall be free.
